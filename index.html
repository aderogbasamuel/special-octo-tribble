What we're building

A marketplace that only works because it's small.

No strangers from across the country, no shipping black holes. Just people on the same campus, trading with each other, protected by an escrow layer that holds funds until a delivery is actually confirmed.

Escrow, not trust falls

Funds sit in escrow until the buyer confirms delivery. No more "did they actually send it" DMs.

Campus-only, by design

Every listing is scoped to a real campus community — you're trading with people you can actually run into.

Built in the open with users

We ship fast, talk to the people using it constantly, and change direction when the data says to.
